# Refurbished vs New Units — Kenya

## Key Highlights

- **Proportion.** 7,443 (8.8%) of 84,952 gross unit sales are refurb units.
- **Trend.** The refurb % was highest in 2026: 2,980 (20.9%) of 14,252 units; over the last 12 full months it is 18.6%.
- **Refunds.** 11.1% of refurb units are refunded vs 9.9% of new units.
- **Selling Agents.** 1,160 (32.9%) of 3,522 Selling Agents have sold at least one refurb unit. The top 10 account for 18% of refurb units, against 11% of all gross unit sales.
- **Days from sale to dispatch.** Refurb: mean 16.4 · median 6 days. New: mean 14.1 · median 6 days. Based on 4,176 refurb and 42,319 new-unit accounts dispatched on or after the sale date (within 365 days).
- **Pending dispatch.** 33 (0.5%) of 6,618 sold refurb units are pending dispatch, vs 142 (0.2%) of 69,819 sold new units.
- **Cash vs PAYG.** Refurb units make up 9.6% of Cash unit sales vs 8.3% of PAYG unit sales.
- **Repeat refurb customers.** 166 (2.3%) of 7,221 customers with a refurb unit have more than one.

## Methodology

**Scope:** Gross unit sales, Kenya: accounts with a sale date plus refunded accounts (category = Unit; latest marts.mart_accounts record per account; test customers excluded)  
**Units:** sum of productQty (a Cash account can carry several units)  
**Refurb units:** products with isRefurb = 1 in amt.products (joined on productId); every other unit is New  
**Dates:** year / month of sale_date; refunded accounts by RefundDate, else the date the account was created  
**People:** Selling Agent = salesAgents on the account, named from employee_name; STL = the Selling Agent's current supervisor in AMT  
**Significance tests:** chi-square and binomial tests on units, 5% level
