# Refurbished vs New Units: Battery vs Direct — Kenya

## Key Highlights

- **Refurb leans towards Battery.** About 1 in 11 units sold is refurb, and it is more common on Battery (about 1 in 10) than on Direct (about 1 in 13). So although most units sold are Direct, Battery makes up 52% of refurb sales against 44% of new sales.
- **Refurb is growing fast.** Over the last 12 months it is about 1 in 4 Battery units and about 1 in 6 Direct units sold, up from about 1 in 11 and about 1 in 20 in 2024.
- **Refurb Direct is refunded more than new Direct, despite being cheaper.** Refurb costs about 36–40% less than new (median PAYG price). Battery: 10.8% of refurb units refunded vs 11.1% of new; Direct: 11.5% of refurb units refunded vs 9.0% of new — so price alone does not seem to explain refunds.
- **In 2026, refurb is being dispatched faster than new.** Refurb units sold this year wait a median 14 days for dispatch vs 21 for new (average 21 vs 33); in 2025 refurb was about as fast as new on the median but slower than new on average (median 7 vs 6, average 15 vs 12). The change comes mainly from new units slowing down (6 → 21 days) more than refurb (7 → 14). Refurb was also quicker in 3 of the 5 earlier years. Only 29% of 2026 sales have a dispatch date recorded so far, so this needs confirming.
- **Battery refurb sells more to Cash buyers; Direct refurb sells more to PAYG buyers.** About 1 in 6 Cash Battery units is refurb; about 1 in 11 PAYG Direct units is refurb. But in 2026 so far, only 1 in 4 refurb units went to Cash buyers — refurb is increasingly reaching PAYG customers.
- **Refurb is a one-off purchase.** Only 1 in 58 refurb customers has bought more than one refurb unit, and about 1 in 13 has also bought a new unit.

## Methodology

**Scope:** Gross unit sales, Kenya: accounts with a sale date plus refunded accounts (category = Unit; latest marts.mart_accounts record per account; test customers excluded)  
**Units:** sum of productQty (a Cash account can carry several units)  
**Refurb units:** products with isRefurb = 1 in amt.products (joined on productId); every other unit is New  
**Product type:** Battery = pump + controller + battery (Climate Smart Battery, CSB); Direct = pump connected straight to the panels (Climate Smart Direct, CSD); from data/product_groups_v2.csv (hand-maintained; products not in it count as Other). Only Battery and Direct products are reported  
**Dates:** year / month of sale_date; refunded accounts by RefundDate, else the date the account was created  
**People:** Selling Agent = salesAgents on the account, named from employee_name; STL = the Selling Agent's current supervisor in AMT  
**Significance tests:** chi-square and binomial tests on units, 5% level  
**Data:** ClickHouse snapshot 27 Oct 2026 18:41 (202610271841)
