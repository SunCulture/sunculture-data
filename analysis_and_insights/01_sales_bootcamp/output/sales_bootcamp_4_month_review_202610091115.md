# Weekly Data Digest — May 2026 Sales Bootcamp: 4-Month Review

**Bootcamp:** 10–13 May 2026 · 155 participants  
**Comparison:** Jan–Apr 2026 (pre) vs Jun–Sep 2026 (post) · May 2026 held out · benchmark: same months in 2025  
**Data:** Sales through 01 Oct 2026 · generated 09 Oct 2026  

## Context

The May 2026 Sales Bootcamp brought together 155 selected participants across Field Sales Agents (FSAs), Telesales, Service Center Associates, Sales Team Leaders (STLs), Regional Sales Managers (RSM) and support functions for four days of sales training, held from 10–13 May 2026.

The June digest reported an early uplift based on the first weeks after training. With four full months of post-training sales now available (Jun–Sep 2026), we have re-run the analysis to see whether that uplift held, how it compares with the same period last year, and what has changed since.

This digest unpacks what changed and who drove it.

## Key Highlights

- **Productivity is up, in a pattern similar to last year.** The Bootcamp cohort moved from 7.7 to 9.7 units per selling agent per month (+26%). Over the same months in 2025, with no bootcamp, the same group rose +24% — a similar pattern that may point to a seasonal component in the lift.
- **Volume grew alongside a larger selling team.** Year on year, Jun–Sep units are +35% while productivity is flat; the cohort had 79 selling agents a month vs 58 last year.
- **The June peak has not held.** Productivity peaked at 11.6 in June; Aug–Sep averaged 8.4, compared with 10.3 in the same months of 2025.
- **SSC shows the strongest improvement.** +33% vs +18% over the same months last year — the segment furthest ahead of its 2025 pattern.
- **Telesales and SSC accounted for most of the growth; Field Sales was flat.** They delivered 96% of added units. Bootcamp Field Sales moved +3%, compared with +46% over the same months in 2025.
- **Bootcamp field agents were strong performers going in — and kept their lead.** Compared with non-bootcamp field selling agents of the same tenure, bootcamp agents out-sold them 2.1× before training and 1.9× after.

## 5. Performance Drivers

- **By segment:** Field Sales +8 units/month (4%), Telesales +93 units/month (50%), SSC +86 units/month (46%) of the cohort's growth.
- **Product mix:** CSD2 + RM2CK(7.5) 5% → 22% of units; Climate Smart Direct 24% → 5% — this may reflect portfolio or stock changes as well as selling behaviour.
- **Lead source:** Refer and Earn remains the main channel (41%, -5 pp); Website grew +4 pp.
- **STL cascade:** 96% of post-bootcamp field units were sold under an STL who attended, so there is no longer a large enough comparison group to confirm the June ~30% finding.
- **Top improvers to recognise:** largest increase in units sold, Jun–Sep 2026 vs Jan–Apr 2026.
